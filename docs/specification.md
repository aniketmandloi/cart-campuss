## Problem Statement

A person researching a car, and what it would take to buy and register one in India, cannot trust a single number that collapses different cars, markets, and prices into one claim. A Model is not a Listing. A manufacturer PriceReference is not dealer stock. An auction guide or a current bid is not a completed sale. An ex-showroom label does not say which charges sit inside the figure. Sales totals and registration stocks are not production counts.

Public pages, marketplaces, and auction houses also do not grant this product a stored worldwide catalogue, a photo library, or a live inventory feed. A calculator that inserts GST, cess, state tax, registration fees, insurance, TCS, customs duty, a market-average loan rate, import eligibility, or a salary requirement would present assumptions as facts. The visitor's problem is to understand a specific car, find a real source, and see what a stated India purchase scenario would cost, with unknowns left unknown.

## Solution

Cart Campuss is a public, anonymous car-research web application with an India buying context. Worldwide completeness is a long-term coverage goal. The first release promises something narrower: understand the car, find the source, and see what a purchase scenario would cost.

Visitors browse a curated catalogue of Brands and Models, select a Generation, SalesMarket, ModelYear, and MarketVariant, and read sourced specifications, honest gaps, and field-level evidence. Market access is either an approved feed of licensed Listing and AuctionLot cards or a clearly labelled SourceDirectory of outbound links. When neither is approved, the page says "No market data". The product does not sell, bid, broker, or take a loan application.

India purchase and finance behavior is quote-assisted. The visitor, or a sourced quote, supplies amounts. The application sums resolved lines, keeps inclusion metadata, and withholds a definitive purchase total while a mandatory line is unresolved. Financing uses the visitor's nominal rate and term, then an explicit rounded amortization schedule. A suggested take-home figure is a labelled budgeting convention. Personal finance inputs stay on the device.

The software can be built and checked against labelled fixtures before any provider contract exists. A public launch still waits for a useful, permission-cleared cohort. An empty name list, a synthetic fixture, or an unpermitted copy is not a substitute for that cohort.

## Implementation Decisions

### Promise and cohort

The first release is a public research product. Visitors browse without an account. The launch claim is the curated cohort that is actually published, not worldwide coverage and not live aggregation.

Curation targets, which are not a reason to invent records, are 10 Brands, 30 distinct Models, and 90 model-year applicability records. The Model target is split into 20 Models offered new in India on the manifest's coverage date and 10 international enthusiast Models that are not offered new in India on that date. India-current assignment wins, so the groups are disjoint. Historical, performance, and limited-production qualities may overlap inside the enthusiast group. A Model is counted once across its trims. Unknown fields are not filled to meet a target. The public manifest states the coverage date, the published counts, the targets, the group breakdown, any shortfall, and whether market access is a SourceDirectory, an approved feed, or both. It claims no minimum Listing count.

A publicly launched applicability record shows identity, SalesMarket, ModelYear, fuel or powertrain, body style, useful core specifications, and source records. An individual core measurement may be unknown with a reason where a reason exists. A names-only page, or a specification section with no sourced facts, is not a public launch record. A missing ProductionClaim does not by itself exclude a useful page. Photos may use the designed fallback. Listings may be absent under the market rules below.

If a useful permission-cleared subset is smaller than the target, the public site may launch that subset and disclose it. If no useful cleared cohort exists, the build stays in private preview. Private preview and automated fixtures may contain incomplete labelled records. Those records are synthetic test data and are not published as real Listings, Vehicles, prices, or quotes.

### Identity

Canonical IDs are stable when public slugs change. Brand, then Model, then Generation, then a market-specific MarketVariant. A model-year applicability record binds a MarketVariant to a ModelYear in a SalesMarket and is the scope for specifications, PriceReferences, comparison, shortlist, and finance. ModelYear stores the manufacturer or market convention that gives the label its meaning. Calendar production year is a different fact.

SalesMarket is the country of specification or official offer. Seller location is separate. RegistrationJurisdiction is the Indian state or union territory the buyer proposes for registration. Country of listing and country of manufacture are separate facts again.

Brand aliases and regional Model names are lookup keys. They do not merge distinct market records. A rebadge is a separate Model and may record an optional relationship to another Model. An uncertain provider match stays unpublished. Fuzzy search never establishes that two Vehicles or MarketVariants are the same.

An incompletely documented or one-off car may be published as a scoped Model or Generation without invented trims or years. Comparison and financial figures wait until an applicability record that the evidence actually supports is selected.

A Vehicle is optional. It is identified only as far as the source permits. A Listing is a separate offer and carries provider identity, external ID, URL, timestamps, geography, condition, and mileage with units. Dealer stock without individual identity does not receive an invented Vehicle or VIN. Public pages may show a source listing or lot ID when the source policy allows it. They do not show a full VIN, a registration number, or private seller contact details. Several Listings may be tied to one Vehicle only after evidence-based matching. That tie is not a warranty from this application. An official PriceReference is not a Listing, and there is no unscoped Model price.

An AuctionEvent records the publisher, sale format, and scheduled or actual end with timezone. An AuctionLot keeps guide, bid, reserve, hammer, and premium semantics independent. A bid is not a sale and is not a final purchase amount.

### Sourced facts, money, and production

Specifications and other claims are sourced-fact records. Each record has a subject and applicability, a property, the raw value, the normalized value, the unit and measurement standard, the source URL or provider key, publication, observation, and effective times where the source has them, retrieval time, reviewer and publication status, and the source-policy reference. Canonical units support filtering. Source units remain visible. PS, bhp, and kW are not interchangeable. NEDC, WLTP, and ARAI results are not interchangeable. Dry, kerb, and gross weights are not interchangeable.

Conflicts remain separate assertions. The curated selection records its reason and source. Search, filters, and comparison use the curated selection. The car page shows the other assertions. An unknown value is an explicit unknown state. It is never stored or displayed as zero.

A ProductionClaim names its Generation or variant scope, geography, period, unit count, whether it is planned or completed, and what the source counted. A sales volume, an export total, a registration stock, or an inferred-sales statistic is not stored as that claim. The page does not attach an industry category total to a Model.

Money is an integer amount in the currency's ISO 4217 minor unit, with the ISO currency, price basis, included and excluded charges, tax-inclusivity metadata, market, and observation date. A converted display keeps the original currency, the rate's source, time, and direction, and the rounding policy. If the minor-unit exponent for a currency is unknown, a scenario in that currency is rejected rather than assumed to use two decimal places.

### Market modes

Launch market access has three public states.

- Approved feed: licensed cards show source, observation time, known lifecycle, original currency, price type, location, and outbound URL. Refresh follows that source's contract. If no permitted freshness interval can support a credible current Listing, the card is suppressed rather than described as live.
- SourceDirectory: the visible label is "Search official and marketplace sources". Entries are reviewed outbound links. An entry has no inventory count, invented Vehicle, copied price, copied photo, location claim, or availability claim. This mode is a valid first public release and is described as a directory in the product and in release notes.
- No market data: not even an appropriate directory is approved.

Listing lifecycle is active as last observed, ended or sold or withdrawn when that end is confirmed, or status unknown. A provider failure preserves known permitted history. It does not become zero supply and it does not become a mass sold or deleted event. Price types stay distinct: asking price, auction guide, current bid, assumed hammer, hammer, buyer premium, completed sale, estimate, and official PriceReference. A reference MSRP is not stock. A current bid or guide cannot be saved as a completed purchase amount. Structured data describes only facts visible on the page and does not emit an offer for a SourceDirectory.

Disputed deep links, framing, and embedding stay disabled. A public URL is not permission to copy the destination. A reviewed ordinary hyperlink may be shown when that use is the one that was reviewed. The first release does not depend on buying a listing or auction feed. CarAPI and MarketCheck are neither selected nor excluded by this specification. A later feed requires a written scope that covers this product, geography, display, retention, attribution, images, and termination or deletion. No such contract is assumed.

### Publishing, freshness, and rights

Each SourcePolicy records permitted display fields and geography, attribution, image permission, cache retention, derivative and aggregation restrictions, refresh interval, permission expiry, and takedown or deletion obligations. Published facts and media reference the applicable reviewed policy. Rights review and factual review are independent gates.

Public reuse is denied until the particular use is reviewed. Facts and images can have different permissions. A public URL, an API subscription, or a press image is not blanket permission. A suspended source cannot add new public results. Expired content is purged or withheld according to its actual terms. Catalogue history may remain visible with its historical date when retention rights allow it.

The publication sequence is import or manual entry, schema validation, identity match, factual and rights review, publish, scheduled recheck, then correct, expire, or withdraw. Provider plus external ID is unique. Ingest runs are idempotent. Records are staged, and the public projection changes in one transaction. A partial failed import is not a deletion. Uncertain matches stay in review.

Freshness stores observed time, fetch time, last successful provider sync, and the next required recheck. The public page shows the observation or review time that supports the displayed value. Review targets are 90 days for editorial specifications, 7 days for public model-level prices, and 30 days for SourceDirectory URLs. Active listing and auction refresh follows the source contract. A missed interval marks the fact stale or not checked and keeps the last review date. It does not blank the fact into zero. Withdrawal removes the prohibited material from pages, caches, and search and writes an audit record.

NHTSA vPIC may be used only as an attributed United States regulatory or VIN reference for a record whose own applicability was reviewed. It is not an India or worldwide catalogue, and it does not supply this product's photographs, Listings, or production totals.

Administration starts as a trusted operator command and reviewed structured seed or import files. It is not a public admin product. Every publishing mutation requires server-verified operator authorization. Visitors have read access only. Provider calls and tokens stay on the server. Public responses exclude secrets, contracts, and private source payloads. There is no public seller-document upload. External links open the reviewed destination without embedding it and without giving that destination access to the originating page. Query and form inputs are validated. Database queries that include those inputs are parameterized. Backups omit provider payloads beyond the retention the source allows. Before public launch, a backup is restored in a staging check.

### Routes and URL state

These routes are the public contract.

- `/` shows prominent search, the Brand index, curated collections, the coverage note, and the RegistrationJurisdiction selector.
- `/brands/{brandSlug}` shows that Brand's Models, grouped by Generation and status, with the filters that apply inside the Brand.
- `/cars` is the shareable filtered catalogue.
- `/cars/{modelSlug}/{generationSlug}` is the car overview. Selected context is carried by canonical IDs, not by display names.
- `/compare` shows two or three applicability records.
- `/saved` shows the browser-local shortlist. Opening this route on another device does not reconstruct the list.

Shareable catalogue parameters are `q`, `brand`, `model`, `generation`, `year`, `market`, `body`, `powertrain`, `transmission`, `coverage`, `page`, and repeated `attr` values. Each `attr` value identifies one sourced attribute and its measurement standard. A price filter is `priceMin` and `priceMax` together with `priceCurrency`, `priceMarket`, `priceBasis`, and `priceUnknown` set to `include` or `exclude`. Feed-only parameters `condition`, `sellerCountry`, `mileageMax`, and `auctionStatus` are honored only while an approved feed is the market mode, and the page states that provider's coverage.

Selected car context uses `market`, `year`, and `variant` as canonical IDs. `section` may be `overview`, `specs`, `market`, `cost`, or `sources`. Omitting `section` lands on the overview without removing the other sections from the document. Omitting the selection IDs leaves the page an overview and does not choose a trim. `registration` may carry the selected RegistrationJurisdiction. A renamed slug resolves to the current slug with the query preserved.

`/compare` accepts two or three `record` parameters. Each value is one model-year applicability ID. `/saved` and `/compare` do not contain loan, income, debt, fee, deposit, or running-cost parameters. Results for the same snapshot and query are ordered deterministically: exact alias matches, then the remaining matches, with a stable identity tie-break. Pagination is part of the shared URL. Back navigation restores the filter URL and the list's scroll position.

The shortlist stores at most 30 distinct applicability IDs on the device. Compare accepts two or three of those records. Saving the shortlist does not write finance or income fields. An explicit local save may retain a CostScenario on the device. If storage is unavailable, the interface says it was not retained and keeps the in-memory scenario for the session.

The correction control opens a new GitHub issue for `aniketmandloi/cart-campuss` with the title and body prefilled from the public Brand, Model, Generation, applicability IDs, and page URL. The prefill contains no finance or income fields. It is not an account system and not a public upload pipeline.

### Car page and visual direction

The page hierarchy is identity, quick facts, section navigation, grouped specifications, market, India cost, and sources. Quick facts cover the applicable powertrain, power, torque, drivetrain, transmission, and acceleration or range or efficiency, each with its measurement standard. A missing quick fact is an em dash with accessible text "not documented".

Desktop shows an adjacent acquisition summary. Smaller screens place that summary after the key facts and offer one "Estimate India cost" anchor. The anchor and any sticky navigation do not cover content or keyboard focus. At mobile widths the layout is one column, the filter UI is a sheet with Apply, Reset, and a result count, and the component breakdown can collapse. The sheet traps focus while open and restores it on close. Buttons and inputs have text labels and WCAG 2.2 AA target sizes. Information is not conveyed by color or hover alone. At 360 CSS pixels and at 200 percent zoom there is no page-level horizontal overflow. A wide comparison uses a labelled scroll region and sticky field labels. Reduced motion is honored. Decorative silhouette images do not receive a model-specific alternative-text claim.

### India purchase accounting

Every CostScenario names its route, RegistrationJurisdiction, date, and assumptions. Country of listing, country of manufacture, SalesMarket, and RegistrationJurisdiction stay distinct. The acquisition total is T. Finance fees are outside T.

Line states are known amount, not applicable with a basis, included elsewhere with a reference, or missing. A blank is missing. Zero is a resolved known amount only when the visitor enters zero or the line records an applicable exemption. Included-elsewhere contributes nothing more to the sum and cites the line that already contains it. Not-applicable contributes nothing and cites its basis. User-entered amounts and not-applicable assertions stay labelled as such.

Domestic-new mandatory lines are the vehicle price, registration or road tax, registration fee, and insurance. The vehicle price is a sourced tax-inclusive ex-showroom reference or an itemized seller PurchaseQuote, and it keeps its basis label. Verified tax-inclusive metadata means those included taxes are not added again. The phrase ex-showroom alone is not that metadata. An on-road or otherwise bundled quote contributes its quoted figure once. Unspecified basis leaves registration, insurance, and seller charges unresolved until a person or a sourced quote resolves them. Optional lines, present only when selected, are dealer accessories, extended warranty, and other named seller charges. A seller-requested fee stays a seller charge. A confirmed discount subtracts once. An additional statutory or collection charge becomes a mandatory line only when the quote or the visitor adds it. The application does not invent one.

Domestic-used mandatory lines are the quoted asking price, transfer or registration, insurance, inspection, and delivery. The used asking price is not rebuilt from a new-car tax template or a dealer taxable margin. Domestic stock of an already imported car uses the matching domestic checklist when its quote and registration context support the domestic route. Customs lines on that route are not applicable, with that context as the basis.

T for a domestic route is the sum of the scenario's known-amount lines, minus confirmed discounts. A line marked included elsewhere or not applicable adds zero. A recoverable or creditable collection is labelled separately from one-time cash outflow. The scenario does not assert that this buyer can recover it. A blank collection line is not treated as tax collected.

Import is a separate itemized scenario. Its named lines are acquisition amount with price type and currency, buyer premium, local seller or auction taxes, export handling, transport, freight, insurance, broker-entered customs, broker-entered IGST, inspection and compliance, Indian registration, Indian insurance, and foreign-exchange or conversion fees. Other charges are optional lines the visitor adds. Auction-specific lines may be resolved as not applicable when the source is not an auction, with that basis recorded. A current bid or guide cannot fill the completed purchase amount. An assumed hammer is an explicitly hypothetical line.

Import evidence status is exactly one of "not reviewed", "documented constraints identified", and "specialist evidence supplied". The route context is an explicit choice among ordinary new, ordinary used, vintage, and other exceptional. Age alone cannot make that choice. None of the evidence statuses certifies that the vehicle can be registered or driven in India. A complete set of money lines does not certify it either. Import results never use the label "Quote-based estimated cash purchase cost" and never show a buy-ready or eligibility-approved total. They state that the sum is not a customs assessment and does not establish registrability.

No automatic GST, compensation cess, state motor-vehicle tax, registration fee, insurance premium, TCS, or customs rate is inserted. No automatic import-eligibility or road-registration decision is made. An attempt to apply an automatic tax rule, including a stale or inapplicable future pack, adds no amount and leaves the scenario quote-assisted. There is no licensed foreign-exchange feed and no international-vehicle loan promise.

A user-supplied exchange rate stores source currency, target currency, direction, rate, and date. It is labelled a conversion assumption. Conversion uses the same decimal precision as the finance engine, rounds half-up to the target minor unit, and keeps the original amount visible. A foreign line without a complete rate stays in its original currency and is unresolved for an INR sum. Conversion does not turn a foreign price into a verified India PurchaseQuote. INR presentation uses Indian digit grouping. The authoritative amount remains the minor-unit integer. A foreign original keeps its own currency's presentation.

Output labels:

- "Known subtotal" when any mandatory line is missing. The missing lines are listed. This includes a partial import worksheet.
- "Hypothetical broker-quote subtotal" for every import scenario. Outstanding steps and unresolved lines are listed. An incomplete import is also a Known subtotal. This caption never upgrades to a buy-ready total.
- "Quote-based estimated cash purchase cost" only for a domestic scenario whose mandatory lines are all resolved and which has a scenario date, a RegistrationJurisdiction, and a vehicle-price basis. It shows that date, jurisdiction, whether each contributing amount is sourced or entered, and the stated exclusions. Missing any of those yields "Known subtotal". The label is not an all-in government assessment and not a guaranteed final payable. Checklist completeness does not mean the seller disclosed every possible statutory liability.
- A definitive final price is withheld whenever a mandatory component is unresolved or the location, date, or price basis is undefined.

Finance calculated from a partial T is an explicit hypothetical. A bounded range is shown only when the components themselves have bounds and those assumptions are shown.

Changing the Vehicle, RegistrationJurisdiction, quote date, or route invalidates incompatible components and snapshots.

### Finance identities and the schedule

The visitor supplies the annual nominal reducing-balance percentage and the term. The term is a whole number of months from 1 through 120 inclusive. The percentage is the number entered: 9 means 9 percent. There is no hidden market-average rate and no lender loan-to-value default. A sourced dated loan quote may be entered and keeps its quoted nominal rate, APR, and fees as separate fields. APR is never substituted for the nominal percentage. A fixed-rate scenario is deterministic. A floating-rate scenario holds the entered percentage constant and says that repayments can change.

Let A be the financed portion of T, Ff the fees financed into the loan, Fc the finance fees paid in cash, and D the deposits already paid. The funding is valid only when `0 ≤ A ≤ T`, `Ff ≥ 0`, `Fc ≥ 0`, and `0 ≤ D ≤ T − A + Fc`. Each finance fee is split between Ff and Fc exactly once and is not included in T. Principal `P = A + Ff`. Empty funding fields mean no finance scenario is shown. They do not default A to T or to zero.

The monthly rate is `r = annual nominal percentage / 1200`. The closed form, computed with decimal arithmetic of at least 28 significant digits, selects the unrounded regular instalment. It does not select the displayed repayment total or interest total.

- When P is 0, the regular instalment is 0 and the mode is cash purchase. The schedule contains no positive payment and no interest. The formula is not divided by the term.
- When r is 0 and P is positive, the unrounded regular instalment is `P / n`.
- Otherwise the unrounded regular instalment is `P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n − 1)`.

Round that unrounded instalment half-up to the loan currency's minor unit. Half-up means a tie rounds away from zero. Call the result R. For each period, multiply the opening balance by the exact r using the same precision, then round that product half-up to the minor unit to get the period interest. When r is 0, period interest is 0. No other intermediate money amount is rounded.

For each period in order:

- When the opening balance is 0, interest is 0, payment is 0, and the closing balance is 0.
- Otherwise the amount due is the opening balance plus the rounded period interest.
- Before the last period, the payment is the lesser of R and the amount due.
- In the last period, the payment is the amount due.
- The closing balance is the amount due minus the payment.

Early clearance therefore produces zero interest and zero payment in every later period. The closing balance of the final period is exactly 0. Total scheduled payments are the sum of the actual payments. Total interest is the sum of the rounded period interest and equals total scheduled payments minus P. Display R as the headline monthly amount. When the last non-zero payment differs from R, display that payment separately. Display total scheduled payments and the finance fees beside the headline. The income formulas use R, not the unrounded closed form and not the different final payment.

Own cash contribution, including deposits, is `T − A + Fc`. Cash still due is `T − A + Fc − D`. Acquisition plus scheduled loan payments is `T − A + Fc + total scheduled payments`. Deposits are already inside own cash and are not added again.

Reject non-finite amounts, negative amounts, monetary inputs with fractions finer than the currency minor unit, funding that breaks the inequalities above, a term that is not a whole number from 1 through 120, and a percentage outside the closed interval from 0 through 36. An empty rate or an empty term produces no EMI and does not borrow a default. An invalid input does not keep a previous result labelled as current.

Each CalculationSnapshot stores the exact inputs, component statuses, formula version `india-scenario-2026-10-22`, rounding policy `minor-unit-half-up-per-period`, tax engine `quote-assisted-none`, any FX snapshot, and the line-item output. Visitor amounts never become published market facts. A change to the acquisition identities, the schedule rounding, the checklist, or the income constraints requires a new formula version. Balloon, lease, variable-payment, arrears, prepayment, and tax-benefit models are absent in this version rather than mapped onto this schedule.

Worked consequences of these identities, in a two-decimal currency:

- T is ₹1,000,000, A is ₹800,000, Ff is ₹10,000, Fc is ₹5,000, and D is ₹50,000. Principal is ₹810,000. Own cash is ₹205,000. Cash still due is ₹155,000. At 0 percent over 60 months every payment is ₹13,500.00, scheduled payments are ₹810,000.00, and interest is ₹0.00.
- P is ₹600,000, the rate is 0 percent, and the term is 60 months. Every payment is ₹10,000.00, scheduled payments are ₹600,000.00, and interest is ₹0.00.
- P is ₹100.00, the rate is 0 percent, and the term is 3 months. R is ₹33.33, the final payment is ₹33.34, scheduled payments are ₹100.00, and interest is ₹0.00.
- P is ₹600,000, the nominal rate is 9 percent, and the term is 60 months. R is ₹12,455.01, the period-60 payment is ₹12,455.32, scheduled payments are ₹747,300.91, interest is ₹147,300.91, and the closing balance is ₹0.00. In minor units those four money results are 1245501, 1245532, 74730091, and 14730091.
- P is ₹3.00, the nominal rate is 2 percent, and the term is 36 months. R is ₹0.09. Period 34 pays ₹0.04 and clears the balance. Periods 35 and 36 pay ₹0.00. Scheduled payments are ₹3.01 and interest is ₹0.01.

These are synthetic specification oracles. They are not lender quotes, and they are not evidence that an application has already produced them.

### Suggested take-home income

The visitor-facing name is "Suggested monthly take-home income for this budget". It is an adjustable budgeting convention. It is not a lender approval, a salary requirement, an observed average salary, or a statement that a purchase is safe. The copy says that living expenses, dependants, emergency reserves, credit profile, actual underwriting, repairs, and resale risk are outside the model.

Let Emi be the regular rounded instalment R. The ratios start at e = 0.15, o = 0.25, and c = 0.40. Each ratio is editable and valid only when it is greater than 0 and no greater than 1. An invalid ratio suppresses the income result and does not silently restore the default for that calculation. O is user-entered monthly running costs and reserves. E is existing debt EMI. O may include fuel or charging, parking or tolls, a maintenance reserve, and a reserve for the next insurance renewal. There are no hidden universal running-cost values. The next-renewal reserve is not added to T. O and E are each either an explicit amount, an explicit none stored as zero, or unknown. Unknown is not coerced to zero.

When no EMI has been produced because the rate or term is empty or invalid, no income result is produced. Cash-purchase mode has an EMI of zero, which is a produced EMI, so the income rules below still apply. A zero EMI does not by itself make the suggested income zero when a known O or E is positive.

Select the maximum of the applicable constraints before presentation rounding, using the finance engine's decimal precision. Round the displayed income upward to the currency minor unit so it does not understate that maximum. Identify each binding constraint before presentation rounding. A smaller component may be explained and is not presented as the suggested income.

- EMI-only mode is always available when an EMI exists. The result is `Emi / e` and it says that ownership costs and existing debt are excluded.
- When O and E are both known, the full result is the maximum of `Emi / e`, `(Emi + O) / o`, and `(Emi + O + E) / c`.
- When E is known and O is unknown, the result is the maximum of `Emi / e` and `(Emi + E) / c`, and it says that running costs are excluded.
- When O is known and E is unknown, the result is the maximum of `Emi / e` and `(Emi + O) / o`, and it says that existing debt is excluded. The combined-commitment term is not evaluated.
- When O and E are both unknown, the only result is the EMI-only result, and it says that both running costs and existing debt are excluded.

An optional entered take-home amount reports the same applicable ratios of that amount. It never says "approved" or "safe to buy". A non-finite, negative, zero, or over-precise take-home amount is rejected because it cannot be a divisor or a money input.

With the default ratios, Emi of ₹5,000, O of ₹10,000, and E of ₹40,000:

- The loan-and-debt result, with running costs excluded, is ₹112,500, set by `(Emi + E) / c`.
- The full result is ₹137,500, set by `(Emi + O + E) / c`.
- Explicit E of zero, with the same Emi and O, gives a full result of ₹60,000, set by `(Emi + O) / o`. The EMI-share constraint remains inside the maximum.

### Privacy

Sensitive finance fields are the nominal percentage, term, financed amounts, fees, deposits, running costs, existing debt, entered take-home income, and the resulting schedule and income figures. They are calculated locally. They are absent from analytics, query strings, server logs, and provider requests. The server sends approved public reference prices and the public checklist definitions only. An explicit device-local save is the only persistence for those fields. A shortlist save does not include them. A CalculationSnapshot that the visitor keeps locally is still not a published market fact.

## Testing Decisions

A good test asserts an observable page, URL, scenario result, validation error, or the absence of a personal field, a secret, or an invented fact. It does not assert helper structure, query text, or CSS class names.

Two seams are the contract.

1. Public discovery-to-detail journey. With labelled fixtures for an India record, an international record, an unknown, a conflict, a withheld field, a SourceDirectory, an approved-feed card, and "No market data", assert search order, the miss state, filter URLs, slug redirect, context selection, sourced details, market labels, comparison and shortlist limits, and correction prefill. Publication is visible on the same journey: an uncertain match stays unpublished, a replayed ingest does not duplicate a Listing, a provider failure preserves history, and a withdrawal disappears from the page, search, and comparison. One car page shows the India workbench, matches the second seam for the same inputs, and does not send those inputs in the request.
2. Cost and finance scenario outputs. Give the pure scenario contract labelled inputs and assert the output label, missing lines, minor-unit amounts, schedule, cash identities, income result, binding constraint, formula version, and rejections. The oracle matrix lives here so personal inputs are not posted to prove the arithmetic. Fixtures stay labelled. The oracles are the worked finance and income examples. Compare exact decimals or minor units, not digit-grouping punctuation. Also assert that a tax-inclusive quote is not taxed again, missing insurance yields "Known subtotal", a bid is not a completed sale, an automatic tax rule adds nothing, a used route skips the new-car template, an import has no buy-ready total, and an empty rate produces no EMI.

The committed starter provides workspace type checks and web/API production-build scripts, but no product journey tests, scenario tests, or configured formatting/lint/test acceptance suite yet. Establish the missing checks in the first implementation slice using existing tools where possible; additional test or formatting dependencies still require approval unless already authorized by the task. This specification update adds no dependency or placeholder test. The oracles are expected results, not results the product has produced. Starter type checks and web/API builds passed when the baseline was committed; native simulator/device behavior, production deployment, and catalogue/cost behavior were not verified by those checks. Design-study interaction checks are not prior test art. Human visual checks, the staging performance profile, and the backup restore are public-launch gates. WCAG 2.2 AA is the bar those checks use. This specification does not say that bar has been met.

## Out of Scope

This specification update adopts the already committed and installed starter and its dependency lockfile. It does not implement the remaining product features, start a server or simulator, deploy, or buy hosting.

Out of scope: native product feature parity and app-store distribution, accounts, sync, alerts, social features, a public admin product, checkout, bidding, seller onboarding, negotiation, brokerage, loan applications, lender referrals, taking deposits, purchasing a car, advertisements, paid membership, affiliate placement, a revenue model, exhaustive worldwide inventory, ownership history, valuation, and depreciation.

Automatic tax and import calculators are out of scope. A later pack needs a jurisdiction, classification, effective interval, source, tax base in the instrument's words, additions, exemptions, an amendment check, verification, and a review deadline. It is not stored now. Also out of scope: a licensed foreign-exchange feed, APR calculation, market-average prices or EMIs, and balloon, lease, variable, arrears, prepayment, and tax-benefit loans.

CarAPI, MarketCheck, and India marketplace or dealer feeds wait on a written permission of the kind above. Scraping, copying protected tables or photographs, and treating the design study's sample car or amounts as catalogue data are out of scope. So are a search cluster, vector database, chat agent, microservices, Redis, a provider-plugin framework, public uploads, geolocation, and invented rows used to hit the 30, 10, or 90 targets.
